PTG · Rec · Week 1

Station-Based Session Plan

4 stations · 7–8 min each · Rotate on coach's signal
1
Passing — Inside of foot
2 × 30 s rounds

Setup

Partners stand 10 yards apart with a small gate (2 cones) between them
Progression
① Unlimited touches
② 2-touch (R)
③ 2-touch (L)
④ 1-touch (closer)
+1 pt for every pass through the gate
⚠️ Don't count if player takes too many touches or misses the gate
2
Ball Mastery
2 × 30 s rounds

5 Skills — pick your round

①Toe taps → advanced: dramatic tap to side (switching feet)
②Croquetas → advanced: add outside foot touch
③Push-pull R/L → advanced: both feet
④Inside outside (B)
⑤Juggling — bounce on ground, touch to start
3
Dribbling Course — finish with shot
2 × 30 s rounds

Setup

2 groups of 3 players, each with 6 poles in a zigzag
P1/P4 dribble bottom to top through the poles and finish with a shot on goal
P2/P3 and P5/P6 wait their turn, then rotate

Dribbling moves at each pole

All right foot
All left foot
Outside foot, then around
Body feint at each pole
Sole roll at each pole
⚡ Competition: race Group A vs Group B — first to score wins!
4
Shooting — finish with coach
Continuous reps

How it works

2 lines: P1–P3 on the left, P4–P6 on the right
P1/P4 give the ball to coach, then make their run
Coach bounces/serves the ball back to shoot
Player shoots, returns to back of line — P2/P5 go next
Coach tip: vary the serve — bouncing pass, ground roll, air feed — to keep players adjusting.
