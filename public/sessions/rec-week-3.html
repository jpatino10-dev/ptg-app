PTG · Rec · Week 3

Station-Based Session Plan

4 stations · 7–8 min each · Rotate on coach's signal
1
Possession — rondo
Continuous

Format — pick by numbers

4v1Small group, tight space
5v2Medium — two defenders working together
7v3Large group, expand the grid
Scoring
Defenders win by stealing the ball or kicking it out
Attackers score with 5 consecutive passes
Coach tip: when defenders win, rotate — one attacker becomes a defender.
2
Passing Square
2 × 30 s rounds

Progressions

①Left foot only — pass around the square
②Right foot only
③Give & go — pass then sprint to next cone; passer follows the ball (L & R)
④Older group: skip 3rd man — skip one corner, play to the far player
Key coaching point: open body to receive, play early, follow your pass.
3
Dribble Skills
2 × 30 s rounds

4 Moves — one per round

①Inside-outside — tap in with inside, push out with outside of same foot
②Step over — swing foot over the ball, explode past with outside
③Scissor — circle around the ball with one foot, exit the other side
④Maradona (drag & spin) — sole drag + 180° turn around the ball
Key: slow on the move, fast out of it. Eyes up after the touch.
4
Turn & Shoot
Continuous reps

How it works

Player faces away from goal; coach plays the ball to their feet
Player receives, turns, and shoots in one fluid motion
Go to back of opposite line after shooting
Turn technique
①Inside foot — pull the ball across your body
②Outside foot — hook turn
③Sole — drag back, spin
④Open inside foot — Cruyff / dummy turn
Coach tip: vary the serve — roll, bounce, air — to keep players adjusting their first touch.
